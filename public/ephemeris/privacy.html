Ephemeris

Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

The short version: nothing leaves your device. Ephemeris has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. Your puzzles, your results, and your location stay on your iPhone.

What Ephemeris stores

Your puzzle results — stars earned, streaks, and nightly records — are stored locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

Ephemeris asks for approximate location permission for one purpose: choosing tonight's constellation from the sky actually above you. The lookup happens entirely on your device. Your location is never stored beyond that calculation, never transmitted, and never shared. If you decline permission, the app simply picks a constellation without your sky and everything else works the same.

What Ephemeris does not do

  • No data collection of any kind — we cannot see how you play, or that you play at all.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers.
  • No accounts, sign-ins, or email addresses.
  • No network requests carrying personal data. Daily puzzles are generated on your device.

Sharing

Share cards (the emoji star grids) are created on your device and go only where you send them, through the standard iOS share sheet. We never see them.

Children's privacy

Ephemeris collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There is nothing to collect, sell, or disclose.

Changes

If a future version of Ephemeris ever changes any of the above — for example, optional cross-device sync through your personal iCloud — this policy will be updated first, and the effective date above will change.
